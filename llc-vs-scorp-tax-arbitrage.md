# Self-Employment Tax Arbitrage: Default LLC vs. S-Corp Election

## Executive Summary
Operating an active business or independent consulting practice under a default entity structure exposes 100% of net profits to self-employment (FICA) taxes. This module models the mechanics of IRS Form 2553 (S-Corporation Election), bifurcating cash flow into a defensible W-2 Officer Salary and tax-exempt K-1 Shareholder Distributions to legally mitigate payroll tax exposure.

## Key Takeaway
If your business nets over $80k, an S-Corp lets you take part of your income as profit instead of salary—completely skipping the 15.3% payroll tax on that portion. On $150k profit, you pocket an extra $9,514 every year, even after paying your accountant.
